Biography

From $100 to a global enterprise.

Khalid “KP” Parekh is a leading IT thought leader and visionary — founder, chairman, and CEO of AMSYS Group.

Known for building healthcare and technology companies from the ground up and scaling them into multimillion-dollar organizations, KP leads with a simple principle: “Our word is our honor, and our honor is our word.”

That principle guided an initial personal investment of just $100 into AMSYS — today a $350-million international enterprise. Under his leadership, AMSYS delivers service and innovation that help clients reduce cost and grow profitability across six verticals: technology, healthcare, insurance, hospitality, energy, and finance.

KP also champions Artificial Intelligence and Cybersecurity — integrating both to future-proof businesses and strengthen digital ecosystems. He brings more than 25 years of experience in management, strategy, sales, operations, analytics, AI, and cybersecurity, and has earned over 40 awards and accolades.

His recognition includes Houston Business Journal’s 40 Under 40 (twice), Fast 100, the HBJ Leadership Award, INC 500/5000, and Fast Tech 50. He was named a finalist for EY Entrepreneur of the Year, serves on multiple boards, and is a member of the NVIDIA Global Enterprise AI Partner Advisory Board.

A featured speaker on radio, television, and at major events — including a TED Talk — KP’s insights have been highlighted in TechCrunch, Yahoo! Finance, American Banker, Fast Company, and CNBC Arabia.
